## 1. Why Weave Exists

### The Loneliness Paradox

We live in the most connected era in human history, yet loneliness has reached epidemic proportions. The U.S. Surgeon General declared loneliness a public health crisis in 2023, estimating its health impact equivalent to smoking 15 cigarettes a day. Young adults -- Millennials and Gen Z -- report the highest rates of loneliness despite being the most digitally connected generations.

The paradox is this: **we have infinite tools for reaching people and zero tools for understanding our relationships with them.**

Social media optimises for impressions. Messaging apps optimise for throughput. Calendar apps optimise for scheduling. None of these tools ask the question that actually matters: *How are your friendships, really?*

### The Invisible Problem

Friendships don't fail dramatically. They fade. A week becomes a month. A month becomes a quarter. One day you realise you haven't spoken to someone who used to be central to your life, and the gap has grown too wide to bridge comfortably. This drift is invisible because:

- There's no metric for "friendship health"
- No one tracks the ebb and flow of social connection
- The mental load of "keeping in touch" sits unstructured in your head
- Guilt accumulates silently until it becomes avoidance

Weave makes the invisible visible. It transforms the unstructured mental burden of "I should reach out to..." into a calm, structured practice.

### Who Weave Is For

Weave is built for **Millennial and Gen Z urban professionals** -- people who:

- Maintain 15-50 meaningful relationships across cities, countries, and life stages
- Value depth over breadth in their connections
- Experience social burnout and decision fatigue about who to prioritise
- Want to be intentional about friendships but lack the cognitive bandwidth
- Feel guilt about letting time slip between catch-ups

Weave is not a social network. It's a **private relationship companion** -- a second brain for the people who matter most.

## 2. The Research Foundation

Weave's intelligence is built on established behavioural science, not feature intuition. Three bodies of research underpin every design decision.

### 2.1 Dunbar's Number (Social Brain Hypothesis)

**Source**: Robin Dunbar, evolutionary psychologist at Oxford University

Dunbar's research demonstrates that humans can maintain approximately **150 meaningful social relationships**, structured in concentric layers of emotional closeness:

| Layer | Size | Emotional Investment | Contact Frequency |
|-------|------|---------------------|-------------------|
| **Support Clique** | ~5 | Highest -- these are your crisis call list | Weekly |
| **Sympathy Group** | ~15 | High -- deep emotional bonds | Bi-weekly |
| **Affinity Group** | ~50 | Moderate -- meaningful but less intimate | Monthly |
| **Active Network** | ~150 | Low -- acquaintances you'd recognise | Quarterly |

**Key insight for Weave**: Each layer has fundamentally different maintenance requirements. A weekly text is appropriate for your inner circle but would feel intrusive for a community acquaintance. Weave's entire scoring and suggestion system is calibrated to these layer-specific expectations.

**Research citations**:
- Dunbar, R.I.M. (1992). "Neocortex size as a constraint on group size in primates." *Journal of Human Evolution*.
- Dunbar, R.I.M. (2010). "How Many Friends Does One Person Need?" Faber & Faber.
- Dunbar, R.I.M. & Shultz, S. (2007). "Evolution in the Social Brain." *Science*, 317(5843).

### 2.2 Relationship Maintenance Theory

**Source**: Canary & Stafford (1994), Oswald et al. (2004)

Research on relationship maintenance identifies five key strategies that sustain close relationships:

1. **Positivity** -- being cheerful and optimistic in interactions
2. **Openness** -- self-disclosure and direct discussion of the relationship
3. **Assurances** -- expressing commitment and care
4. **Social networks** -- involving mutual friends and family
5. **Task sharing** -- sharing responsibilities and activities

Weave's interaction categories (deep-talk, meal-drink, activity-hobby, favor-support, celebration, etc.) map directly to these maintenance strategies. The scoring system rewards diverse maintenance behaviours rather than optimising for any single strategy.

### 2.3 Social Health & Wellbeing

**Sources**: Holt-Lunstad et al. (2010), Cacioppo & Patrick (2008)

- Social isolation increases mortality risk by **26%** (Holt-Lunstad meta-analysis of 148 studies)
- Quality of relationships matters more than quantity -- five deep friendships outperform fifty shallow ones
- **Social energy is finite** -- research on introversion/extroversion (Cain, 2012) shows that people have different social capacities that fluctuate over time
- Relationship satisfaction correlates with **intentionality** -- people who deliberately invest in friendships report higher wellbeing (Demir & Weitekamp, 2007)

**Key insight for Weave**: Social wellness is not about maximising social activity. It's about **allocating limited social energy wisely** based on current capacity and relationship needs. This is why Weave includes the Social Battery and Social Season systems -- they respect that your capacity changes.

### 2.4 Habit Formation Science

**Sources**: Clear (2018), Fogg (2019), Duhigg (2012)

Weave's engagement model follows established habit loop principles:

| Habit Element | Weave Implementation |
|---------------|---------------------|
| **Cue** | Gentle notification: "It's been a while since you connected with Sarah" |
| **Routine** | Log the interaction (designed to take <30 seconds) |
| **Reward** | Visible score increase, insight receipt, pattern recognition |
| **Investment** | Richer data makes future suggestions more personalised |

The key is that Weave makes the *intrinsically* rewarding (deepening friendships) also *visibly* rewarding (the app reflects your effort back to you). This is the difference between a fitness tracker that counts steps and a therapist who says, "I've noticed you exercise more when you're stressed."

## 3. Core Product Philosophy

### The Five Principles

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Insight over Achievement** | The reward is understanding your relationships, not collecting trophies |
| **Narrative over Metrics** | Your friendships tell stories, not produce dashboards |
| **Gentle over Urgent** | Observations, not judgments. Suggestions, not demands |
| **Quality over Quantity** | Depth of connection matters more than interaction count |
| **Invisible Intelligence** | The most sophisticated mechanics are the ones the user never sees |

### What "Mindful" Means

Weave uses the word "mindful" deliberately. It doesn't mean slow, passive, or meditative. It means **intentional**. Weave helps you move from:

- **Reactive socialising** ("Oh, I should text them back") to **proactive connection** ("This is the right week to reach out to Marcus")
- **Guilt-driven outreach** ("I feel bad I haven't called") to **insight-driven investment** ("Sarah's going through something -- a check-in would mean a lot right now")
- **Cognitive overload** ("I have so many people to keep up with") to **structured clarity** ("These three people need my energy this week")

### The Engagement Loop

Weave's engagement model is fundamentally different from social media:

```
1. User logs interaction with a friend
2. App observes patterns over time
3. App reflects insights back ("I noticed...")
4. User feels understood, not rewarded
5. User returns because the app sees them clearly
```

This is the difference between a **badge** ("50 interactions! Good job!") and an **insight** ("You and Sarah always connect deeper when you do activities together rather than just catching up over coffee. Your Hermit friendship thrives on shared silence.").

## 5. Pillar 1: Dunbar's Layers

### The Structure

Weave implements three of Dunbar's concentric circles as its primary organisational framework:

| Layer | Name | Capacity | Decay Rate | Ideal Frequency | What It Means |
|-------|------|----------|-----------|-----------------|--------------|
| **1** | Inner Circle | 5 people | 2.5 pts/day | Weekly | Your crisis call list. The people you'd cross a city for at 2 AM. |
| **2** | Close Friends | 15 people | 1.5 pts/day | Bi-weekly | Cherished bonds that enrich your life. You share real vulnerability here. |
| **3** | Community | 50 people | 0.4 pts/day | Monthly | Meaningful acquaintances. You care about each other but don't depend on each other daily. |

### Why Capacity Limits Matter

Weave enforces hard capacity limits (5 / 15 / 50) because the science is clear: **humans cannot maintain unlimited close relationships.** Pretending otherwise leads to the exact guilt and overwhelm Weave aims to solve. The limits force a healthy question: *Who really belongs in my inner circle right now?*

### Tier-Aware Intelligence

Every system in Weave calibrates its behaviour to the tier:

- **Decay**: Inner Circle friends decay fastest (2.5 pts/day) because these relationships require the most frequent maintenance. Community friends decay slowly (0.4 pts/day) because monthly check-ins are sufficient.
- **Drifting thresholds**: An Inner Circle friend is flagged as drifting below 50 points. A Community friend isn't flagged until below 20. The expectations scale with the intimacy level.
- **Suggestions**: Inner Circle drift triggers urgent, non-dismissible suggestions. Community drift is a gentle nudge.
- **Notifications**: The system sends fewer, softer reminders about Community friends and more proactive alerts about Inner Circle drift.

### Tier Fit Analysis

Weave doesn't just let you assign tiers -- it learns whether the tier matches reality. After 5+ interactions, the system analyses your actual contact frequency against tier expectations:

- Connecting with a Community friend weekly? Weave might suggest promoting them to Close Friends.
- Only reaching out to an Inner Circle friend monthly? Weave gently suggests reconsidering the tier -- not as a judgment, but as alignment.

The goal is **congruence between how you categorise a friendship and how you actually invest in it.**

## 6. Pillar 2: Tarot Archetypes

### The Philosophy

While Dunbar's layers answer **how much** energy a friendship needs, archetypes answer **what kind** of energy. Not all friendships are the same. The friend you call during a crisis is fundamentally different from the friend you call for an adventure. Weave's archetype system gives each friendship a personality.

The tarot framing is deliberate: it provides a **symbolic, evocative language** for something most people struggle to articulate -- "What makes this friendship unique?" Rather than clinical labels (attachment styles, personality types), tarot archetypes offer warmth, mystique, and intuitive resonance. They make the act of classifying a friendship feel like discovery rather than diagnosis.

**Design principle**: The tarot framing earns its place in the *results and personality*, not the mechanics. The user doesn't need to know tarot -- they just need to recognise: "Yes, my friendship with Marcus is definitely an adventure. He's my Fool."

### The Eight Archetypes

| Archetype | Essence | Energy | How They Connect | Care Style |
|-----------|---------|--------|-----------------|------------|
| **The Sun** | Optimist & Energiser | Celebration, shared joy | Group activities, events, bringing people together | Celebration & Energy |
| **The Hermit** | Guide & Truth-Seeker | Quality one-on-one time | Deep conversations, parallel silence, walks | Presence & Wisdom |
| **The Emperor** | Protector & Builder | Structure, mutual respect | Consistency, scheduled meetups, reliability | Acts of Service |
| **The Fool** | Adventurer & Joy-Bringer | Spontaneity, adventure | Novel experiences, surprises, saying yes | Play & Adventure |
| **The Empress** | Nurturer & Creator | Warmth, care | Cooking together, comfort, emotional support | Gifts & Nurturing |
| **The Magician** | Catalyst & Inspirer | Creativity, collaboration | Projects, brainstorming, making things together | Shared Projects |
| **The High Priestess** | Intuitive & Confidant | Depth, intuition | Meaningful conversation, emotional truth-telling | Deep Listening |
| **The Lovers** | Mirror & Harmoniser | Connection, harmony | Quality time, shared values, mutual reflection | Quality Time |

### How Archetypes Shape Everything

Archetypes are not cosmetic labels. They fundamentally influence every algorithm in the app:

#### Scoring Multipliers

Each archetype has different affinities for interaction types. The scoring engine applies a matrix of multipliers (0.2x to 2.2x) that reward interactions aligned with the archetype's nature:

| | Deep Talk | Event/Party | Activity | Meal | Celebration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hermit** | 2.0x | 0.2x | 1.2x | 1.2x | 0.5x |
| **Sun** | 1.2x | 2.2x | 1.8x | 1.5x | 2.2x |
| **High Priestess** | 2.2x | 0.7x | 1.0x | 1.2x | 1.0x |
| **Fool** | 0.8x | 1.8x | 2.0x | 1.5x | 2.0x |
| **Emperor** | 0.8x | 1.8x | 1.8x | 1.2x | 1.5x |

**What this means**: A deep conversation with your Hermit friend earns 2x points because that's how Hermit friendships thrive. Dragging your Hermit to a party earns almost nothing (0.2x) because that's not what this friendship is about. The app gently guides you toward nurturing each friendship *in the way it naturally wants to be nurtured*.

#### Decay Modifiers & Grace Periods

Different archetypes tolerate different gaps between contact:

| Archetype | Decay Speed | Grace Period | Why |
|-----------|------------|--------------|-----|
| **Hermit** | 0.5x (half speed) | 14 days | Deep bonds don't need constant contact. Hermit friendships are patient. |
| **High Priestess** | 0.7x | 10 days | Intuitive connections trust the silence between conversations. |
| **Magician** | 0.8x | 7 days | Creative partnerships need regular sparks but not daily ones. |
| **Fool** | 0.8x | 7 days | Adventurers pick up where they left off easily. |
| **Empress** | 0.9x | 7 days | Nurturing bonds thrive on consistent presence. |
| **Emperor** | 1.0x (full speed) | 5 days | Structured friendships need regular touchpoints to stay on track. |
| **Lovers** | 1.0x | 5 days | Deep alignment requires frequent calibration. |
| **Sun** | 1.0x | 5 days | Energy-sharing friendships need regular recharging. |

**Grace period**: No decay is applied during the grace window. A Hermit friend won't start fading until 14 days of silence -- because that's how those friendships actually work. An Emperor friend starts fading after just 5 days, because structure-oriented friendships need regularity.

#### Group Interaction Dynamics

Three archetypes are **group-immune** -- they receive full points even in large group settings:

- **The Sun** (thrives on group energy)
- **The Lovers** (finds connection even in crowds)
- **The Magician** (catalyses creative group dynamics)

All other archetypes experience logarithmic group dilution. A hangout with 4 people gives each friend ~74% of the solo interaction value. A party with 15 gives ~49%. This reflects the reality that individual attention is diluted in groups -- *unless* the friendship archetype specifically thrives in that dynamic.

#### Archetype-Specific Suggestions

When the system generates suggestions, it speaks the language of the archetype:

- **Hermit drifting**: "This friendship rests easily, but a quiet check-in could be nice."
- **Sun drifting**: "Your usual rhythm has gone quiet. Time for some shared joy?"
- **Fool thriving**: "You and Alex are finding your groove. Plan an adventure?"
- **Empress milestone**: "Prepare a thoughtful gift or homemade gesture."
- **Emperor milestone**: "Organise a milestone celebration with structure."

### The Archetype Quiz

Users can discover their own archetype through an 8-question scenario-based quiz. The quiz is designed to feel like self-discovery, not a test:

- Questions are relatable and practical ("It's Friday evening. You feel most recharged...")
- No wrong answers -- every archetype is valid
- Takes 60-90 seconds
- Uses a weighted slider for nuanced results (not binary choices)
- Result reveals primary + secondary archetype

The quiz also *teaches the philosophy* through its questions. By the end, users intuitively understand what archetypes mean, even if they've never encountered tarot.

### Archetype Compatibility

When two friends are both on Weave (linked profiles), the system calculates compatibility scores:

| Pairing | Compatibility | Why |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| High Priestess + Hermit | 0.95 | Both value depth and intimate connection |
| Fool + Sun | 0.90 | Spontaneous joy meets celebratory energy |
| Emperor + Empress | 0.90 | Structure meets nurturing -- complementary care styles |
| Hermit + Sun | 0.40 | Different rhythms -- one needs silence, the other needs energy |
| Hermit + Fool | 0.30 | Withdrawal meets chaos -- difficult but not impossible |

These compatibility insights help users understand *why* certain friendships feel effortless and others require more intentional navigation.

## 7. Pillar 3: Social Seasons

### The Concept

Social energy is not constant. You have weeks where you're bursting with social enthusiasm and weeks where you need to cocoon. Most apps ignore this reality -- they push notifications and suggestions at the same rate regardless of your capacity.

Weave introduces **Social Seasons**: three phases that adapt the entire system to your current energy level.

| Season | Energy State | What Happens |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| **Resting** | Low energy, recovering | Fewer suggestions. Slower decay (relationships wait for you). +20% bonus on any interaction you *do* manage. Gentler language. |
| **Balanced** | Sustainable pace | Standard suggestions and decay. The baseline experience. |
| **Blooming** | High energy, growth phase | More proactive suggestions. Quality-focused bonuses (+10% for great vibes). Slightly faster decay on close ties (nudges you to invest while you have energy). |

### How Seasons Are Determined

Seasons are calculated from two weighted inputs:

**Activity Score (60%)**:
- How often you're logging interactions (3-4 per week is the target)
- Monthly consistency (sustained patterns, not bursts)
- Average friend health across your network
- Active momentum with specific friends

**Battery Score (40%)**:
- Your Social Battery check-in average (a daily 1-5 energy rating)
- Battery trend (rising, stable, or falling)

### The Social Battery

The Social Battery is a daily wellness check-in that takes 2 seconds:

> "How's your social energy today?" [Low] [Okay] [High]

This simple input drives powerful adaptations:

- **Notification frequency**: Low battery = fewer, gentler nudges
- **Suggestion urgency**: Low battery = only show critical suggestions (an Inner Circle friend in crisis)
- **Season calculation**: Sustained low battery can shift you into Resting season
- **Dashboard tone**: Language shifts from "You've been crushing it!" to "Take the time you need."

### Burnout Prevention (Circuit Breaker)

The system includes hard overrides for extreme states:

- **Battery avg < 1.8**: Forces Deep Resting regardless of activity (overrides all other signals)
- **Battery avg < 2.5**: Caps at Balanced (can't enter Blooming even with high activity)

This prevents the scenario where someone is pushing through burnout -- logging interactions despite being exhausted -- from receiving *more* suggestions because the activity score is high.

### Season-Aware Decay

This is where Seasons become genuinely compassionate:

**During Resting**: Decay slows by 20-30%. Your friendships *wait* for you. An Inner Circle friend that would normally lose 2.5 pts/day only loses 1.75. The app is saying: "It's okay to step back. Your people will still be here."

**During Blooming**: Decay increases slightly for close ties (20% faster for Inner Circle). This isn't punishment -- it's a nudge. While you have abundant energy, invest it in your closest relationships. Community tier decay stays the same -- low-maintenance friendships don't need extra pressure.

## 8. The Intelligence Engine

### Overview

The intelligence engine is the brain of Weave. It runs silently in the background, processing every interaction to maintain a living, breathing model of each friendship.

### The Scoring System

When you log an interaction ("I had coffee with Sarah for 2 hours, it was great"), the scoring engine calculates points through a sophisticated multi-factor formula:

```
Points = Base Score
       x Archetype Multiplier     (0.2x - 2.2x)
       x Duration Modifier        (0.8x - 1.2x)
       x Vibe Multiplier          (0.8x - 1.5x)
       x Group Dilution Factor    (0.25x - 1.0x)
       x Event Multiplier         (1.0x - 1.5x)
       x Quality Multiplier       (0.7x - 1.3x)
       x Multi-Day Multiplier     (1.0x - 2.5x)
       x Momentum Bonus           (1.0x or 1.15x)
       + Olive Branch Bonus       (+30 for reconnection)
```

**What each factor captures**:

| Factor | What It Measures | Example |
|--------|-----------------|---------|
| **Base Score** | Inherent value of the interaction type | Deep talk (28 pts) vs. text (10 pts) |
| **Archetype Multiplier** | How well-matched this interaction is to the friendship's archetype | Hermit + deep talk = 2.0x. Hermit + party = 0.2x |
| **Duration** | Time invested | Quick check-in (0.8x) vs. extended hangout (1.2x) |
| **Vibe** | Emotional quality (expressed as moon phases) | Full Moon / magical (1.5x) vs. New Moon / difficult (0.8x) |
| **Group Dilution** | Individual attention in group settings | 4 friends = 0.74x. Immune for Sun/Lovers/Magician |
| **Event Multiplier** | Special occasions | Birthday (1.3x), wedding (1.5x), crisis support (1.4x) |
| **Quality** | Depth of engagement + energy level | Detailed reflection + high vibe = 1.3x |
| **Multi-Day** | Extended time together (trips, visits) | 7-day trip = 2.35x (square root curve prevents gaming) |
| **Momentum** | Consistent recent contact | +15% if you've been in regular touch |
| **Olive Branch** | Reconnecting after drift | Flat +30 pts for reaching out to a dormant friendship |

**Design philosophy**: The formula rewards *meaningful* connection, not *frequent* connection. A single deep conversation with your Hermit friend can earn more than five quick texts with your Emperor friend -- because that's what the friendship values.

### The Decay System

Relationships don't stay static. Without maintenance, they naturally fade. Weave models this through a layered decay system:

```
Daily Decay = Base Tier Rate
            x Archetype Modifier    (0.5x - 1.0x)
            x Decay Zone Modifier   (0.15x - 1.0x)
            x Resilience Modifier   (0.67x - 1.25x)
            x Season Multiplier     (0.7x - 1.2x)
```

**Decay Zones** (a breakthrough design decision):

As a friendship score gets lower, decay *slows down*. Friendships don't die -- they go dormant.

| Score Range | Decay Speed | What This Means |
|-------------|------------|-----------------|
| 50+ | Full (100%) | Healthy territory. Normal pressure to maintain. |
| 30-49 | Reduced (70%) | Drifting. Decay slows. A gentle nudge to reconnect. |
| 15-29 | Crawling (40%) | Cooling. Almost no decay. The friendship is settling. |
| 0-14 | Near-zero (15%) | Dormant. The friendship is resting, not dead. Always something to rekindle. |

**Why this matters**: A friendship at score 10 will *stay* at score 10 for months. It won't race to zero. This matches emotional reality -- a friendship you haven't tended in six months isn't gone. It's dormant. And when you reach out, the Olive Branch bonus (+30 pts) makes the reconnection feel powerful.

### Resilience: The Depth of Your Bond

Resilience is an invisible quality of each friendship that measures **how well the bond weathers time gaps**.

- Built through consistently positive interactions (+0.008 per good vibe)
- Slightly eroded by negative interactions (-0.005 per bad vibe)
- Range: 0.8 (fragile) to 1.5 (unshakeable)

**What high resilience means**: A friendship with 1.5 resilience decays 33% slower. You could go three weeks without contact and barely notice the score change. This models real life -- some friendships can pick up exactly where they left off after months of silence.

**What low resilience means**: A friendship with 0.8 resilience decays 25% faster. This is a newer or more volatile connection that needs frequent maintenance to stay strong.

**Asymmetric by design**: It's easier to build resilience than to lose it. Good experiences compound; bad ones are absorbed.

### Momentum

Momentum tracks **active engagement velocity**. When you're in a good rhythm with someone -- meeting regularly, having great conversations -- momentum builds and grants a 15% scoring bonus.

But momentum decays at 1 point per day. Miss a week, and the bonus fades. This creates a gentle incentive to maintain rhythm without punishing occasional gaps.

## 9. The Journal: Your Relationship's Memory

### Philosophy

> The journal is not a diary -- it's the **sensory organ** that feeds the intelligence engine. Every entry makes the app smarter about your relationships.

Traditional journaling apps optimise for capturing *moments*. Weave's journal optimises for understanding *relationships*. The difference:

| Traditional Journal | Weave Journal |
|--------------------|---------------|
| "Had coffee with Sarah today" | "Had coffee with Sarah. She mentioned the career change again -- third time this month. Something is shifting." |
| Entry stored as text | Entry analysed: themes extracted, threads tracked, memories bridged, actions suggested |
| Value comes from re-reading | Value comes from the system *understanding* what you wrote |

### The Intelligence Pipeline

When you save a journal entry, this happens silently:

```
Entry Saved
  |
  ├─> Signal Extraction: Sentiment, themes, dynamics, confidence
  |
  ├─> Thread Tracking: Ongoing conversation topics per friend
  |     (e.g., "Sarah's career change" -- mentioned 3x this month)
  |
  ├─> Memory Bridge: 4-8 memory candidates generated per friend
  |     (e.g., "Remember: Sarah is considering the startup offer")
  |
  ├─> Action Extraction: Smart actions surfaced
  |     (e.g., "Plan a supportive catch-up", "Note her decision deadline")
  |
  └─> Friend Intelligence Updated: Sentiment, mention count, themes
```

### The Insight Receipt

After you save an entry, the system doesn't just say "Saved!" It shows an **Insight Receipt** -- a brief, animated card that reveals what the system understood:

- Theme pills: "Themes: vulnerability, career change, growth"
- Thread connection: "Connects to an ongoing thread: Sarah's career transition"
- Smart actions: [Remember her deadline] [Plan supportive catch-up]

This closes the feedback loop. The user thinks: "The app actually understood what I wrote." That's the engagement driver -- not points or badges, but the feeling of being *heard*.

### Thread Continuity

Weave tracks **conversation threads** across journal entries. If you mention Sarah's career change in three entries over two months, the system recognises this as a thread and:

- Shows it as a contextual chip in your next reflection about Sarah
- Prompts follow-up questions: "Last time, Sarah was weighing the startup offer. Has anything changed?"
- Flags it for relationship insights if the sentiment is concerning

This transforms isolated journal entries into a **narrative arc** for each friendship.

### Story Chips

Story Chips are pre-built building blocks for reflection. Rather than staring at a blank page, users can construct entries from contextual chips:

- **Activity**: "shared a meal", "went for a walk"
- **Feeling**: "connected", "inspired", "bittersweet"
- **Dynamic**: "shared equally", "I opened up"
- **Moment**: "laughed so hard", "had a breakthrough"

The system learns which chips you use most with each friend and surfaces insights: "You use 'breakthrough' with Sarah but 'laughed so hard' with Marcus. Different energies, both valuable."

## 10. Suggestions, Prompts & Habit Formation

### The Suggestion Engine

Weave generates personalised suggestions through a **priority waterfall** of 13 specialised generators:

| Priority | Generator | When It Fires | Example |
|----------|-----------|--------------|---------|
| **1** | Planned Weave | Past-due or upcoming plans | "Did you meet with Sarah yesterday?" |
| **2** | Life Event | Birthday/anniversary today or soon | "Marcus's birthday is tomorrow!" |
| **3** | Drift (Critical) | Inner Circle < 30 points | "Reconnect with Jamie" (non-dismissible) |
| **3** | Reflection | Interaction logged but no reflection | "How was your time with Alex?" |
| **4** | Drift (High) | Tier threshold breached | "Plan time with Sarah" |
| **5** | Life Event (Upcoming) | Birthday within 7 days | "Sarah's birthday is next Friday" |
| **6** | Momentum | Active streak + high score | "Ride the wave! Keep it going with Marcus!" |
| **7** | Maintenance | Regular check-in due | "Check in with Chris" |
| **8** | Deepen | Score > 85 (thriving) | "Celebrate! This bond is flourishing!" |
| **10** | Reciprocity | Imbalanced initiation | "Let them reach out this time" |
| **12** | Insight | Pattern-based recommendation | "Try a different approach with this friend" |

### Dismissal Learning

The system learns from what you dismiss:

- Dismiss a friend's suggestions 3+ times in 30 days? That friend is suppressed for a month.
- Dismiss a suggestion type 6+ times? Its urgency is downgraded.
- Every "not now" teaches the system what you don't want.

### Notification Intelligence

Notifications are treated as a **limited resource**, not a growth hack:

| Channel | Frequency | Battery-Aware | Configurable |
|---------|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| Battery Check-in | Daily (configurable time) | N/A | Time, enable/disable |
| Smart Suggestions | Max daily budget | Yes -- Low battery = critical only | Frequency tier |
| Deepening Nudges | Max 2/day, 4-8h after interaction | Yes | Enable/disable |
| Weekly Reflection | 1x/week (configurable day) | No | Day, time |
| Evening Digest | Daily (configurable time) | No | Enable/disable |

**Quiet hours** are enforced. **Battery level** gates notification volume. **Jitter** prevents predictable notification patterns that users learn to ignore.

### Habit Formation Design

Weave builds social connection habits through layered reinforcement:

1. **Cue**: Morning battery check-in ("How's your energy?") primes social awareness
2. **Micro-action**: Logging an interaction takes <30 seconds
3. **Immediate reward**: Score increase + Insight Receipt + pattern recognition
4. **Delayed reward**: Weekly reflection shows cumulative progress
5. **Variable reward**: Proactive insights appear unpredictably, creating discovery
6. **Streak reinforcement**: Weekly streak counter visible on dashboard
7. **Adaptive pressure**: System backs off when you're tired, pushes when you're energised

The result: **a daily practice of social intentionality** that feels natural rather than forced.

## 11. The Oracle: AI-Powered Relationship Wisdom

### What the Oracle Is

The Oracle is Weave's AI-powered reflection partner. It's not a chatbot -- it's a **counsellor you consult with purpose**. The distinction matters:

| Chatbot | Oracle |
|---------|--------|
| Open-ended conversation | Bounded, purposeful exchanges |
| Generic advice | Insights grounded in YOUR data |
| Always available, always talking | Consulted intentionally, speaks when it has something meaningful to say |

### Oracle Modes

When users invoke the Oracle (from a journal entry, friend profile, or dedicated tab), they choose from intent-based modes:

| Mode | Purpose | Internal Archetype | What It Does |
|------|---------|-------------------|-------------|
| **Go Deeper** | Understand a moment in context | High Priestess | Leads with analysis, surfaces patterns, cites data |
| **Plan Next Steps** | Turn reflection into action | Emperor | Extracts actionable items, offers one-tap execution |
| **Expand Entry** | Capture more with AI assistance | Magician | Guided Q&A to enrich a journal entry |
| **Quick Actions** | Navigate with context | N/A | Direct navigation to app features, pre-filled |

### Proactive Insights

The Oracle doesn't just respond -- it **observes**. When patterns emerge, it surfaces cards:

- **Friend insights**: "You haven't mentioned Sarah in three weeks, but you used to write about her weekly."
- **Pattern insights**: "You connect most deeply on weekday evenings. Weekend plans tend to feel more surface-level."
- **Milestone insights**: "This is your 50th interaction with Marcus. Here's the arc of your friendship."

These insights are:
- **Capped at 3** (never an overwhelming inbox)
- **Time-limited** (24-48 hour expiry -- miss it, it's gone)
- **Auto-dismissed** when the situation changes (log a weave with Sarah, and her drift insight disappears)
- **Pull, not push** (discovered on the Oracle tab, never sent as notifications)

## 13. What Weave Is Not

| Weave Is NOT | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| A social network | No profiles, no feeds, no followers. Your data is yours. |
| A messaging app | We don't replace how you communicate. We help you decide *when* and *with whom*. |
| A contact manager | We don't store contact details as a primary function. We model *relationships*. |
| A guilt machine | Suggestions are gentle. Notifications respect your energy. The app adapts to you, not the other way around. |
| A gamification app | No XP. No leaderboards. No "50 interactions -- unlocked Gold tier!" Badges exist but are being replaced by narrative insights. |
| A replacement for therapy | We help you understand your social patterns. We don't diagnose or treat. |
